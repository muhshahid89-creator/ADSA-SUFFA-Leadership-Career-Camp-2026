THE MISSION

Higher Studies &
Leadership Camp

As our students from SUFFA Darses step into the next phase of their academic journey for higher studies, ADSA presents an intensive 7-Day Residential Camp. This camp is specially designed to equip outgoing students with essential life skills, leadership qualities, career clarity, and spiritual resilience. Through interactive workshops, expert talks, and mentorship sessions, we aim to bridge traditional Islamic scholarship with modern academic excellence.
