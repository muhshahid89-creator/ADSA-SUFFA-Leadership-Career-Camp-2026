ELIGIBILITY CRITERIA

Candidate Qualifications

To ensure an enriching and focused environment, participants must satisfy the following admission guidelines.

Branch Dars Student

Must be a currently enrolled regular student in one of the 41 affiliated Dars communities across Kerala.

Usthad Recommendation

Must obtain recommendation and permission from the respective Dars Sadar Usthad / Mudarris.

Eligible Class Levels

Open for students studying in Oola, Saniya, Mutawassitha, and Aaliyah academic levels.

Attendance

This ensures academic continuity. It means the student cannot skip any yearly board, semester, or final exams; they must have taken every mandatory exam leading up to the current level or qualification.

ADSA SUFFA CAMP 2026

State-level leadership and career residential camp for students, organized under the leadership of the Alathurpadi Dars Students Association (ADSA).
